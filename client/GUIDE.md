# Audit Management System — Workflow Guide

**A practical manual for everyone who uses the platform.**

This guide tells you how real work gets done here: how an audit is planned, executed, reviewed, reported and followed up — and who does what at each step. It is written for working use, not for reading cover to cover. Find your role in Section 3, go to the workflow you need, and follow the steps.

Everything described here can be done in the app as shipped. The same thirteen workflow documents are also available inside the application under **Project Guide → Workflows**, where you can open each one, print it, or download the whole set as PDF or Word.

## 1. How this guide works

Each workflow in Section 4 is a numbered procedure. You do the steps in order; most take a minute or two. Every step is tagged so you know what kind of step it is before you take it:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Navigate** | Go to a page or open a tab. |
| **Perform** | Do something — click, fill, save. |
| **Watch / Check** | Look at what the system did in response. These steps are how you confirm the workflow is working. |
| **Switch role** | Sign out and continue as a different account. |
| **Good to know** | Background that explains why a step exists. |

Steps marked **Tip** are shortcuts worth knowing but safe to skip.

Whenever a procedure says "sign in as …" it refers to the ready-made accounts on the sign-in page. All of them use the same password, which you can change any time under My Profile.

## 2. The audit lifecycle at a glance

Every engagement in the platform moves through the same five stages. The names matter — statuses, permissions and notifications all key off them.

```
PLANNING → EXECUTION → REVIEW → REPORTING → COMPLIANCE FOLLOW-UP
```

- **Planning.** The annual plan is built in Audit Planning: engagements are created, a lead and a team are assigned (or a saved team is loaded), and the calendar, risk scores and resource load update as you go.
- **Execution.** The audit workspace is where fieldwork happens — checklist, evidence, findings, observations, work papers and team discussion, all under one roof with a full action history.
- **Review.** Work papers are approved, the audit is submitted for review, approved, and finalised. Reviewers can be nudged with a reminder if a review sits idle.
- **Reporting.** A report is generated from a template, moves through Draft → In Review → Approved → Final, and is exported as PDF, Word, CSV or Excel.
- **Compliance follow-up.** Observations carry due dates. Departments respond; the platform reminds, escalates and records every single action until the observation is resolved.

Nothing in between is lost: every action — every checklist tick, upload, remark, reminder and approval — is recorded in the audit's timeline and in the central activity trail.

## 3. Roles and what they own

Six roles cover the whole function. The platform shows each person only what their role needs, so two people rarely see the same app.

| Role | Owns in practice | Used in workflows |
|---|---|---|
| **System Administrator** | Accounts, permissions, settings, data backups, integrations | Everything (full access) |
| **Audit Director** | Final approvals, finalising audits, portfolio oversight | Reviews, reports, teams & departments |
| **Chief Audit Officer** | The audit plan, quality review, escalation decisions, reminders | The core loop (W1–W10) |
| **Senior Audit Officer** | Leads engagements: checklists, evidence, findings, reminders | Execution, findings, review nudge |
| **Junior Audit Officer** | Checklist items, evidence uploads, discussion | Execution, discussion |
| **Department Coordinator** | The department's side: responses to findings, compliance follow-up | Findings response, observation follow-up |

If a button is missing for you, it is usually because your role does not need it — or because an administrator switched that permission off (Users & Roles → Roles & Permissions). Permissions are live: the moment one changes, the buttons appear or disappear for everyone in that role.

## 4. Everyday workflows

The thirteen procedures below cover the platform end to end. Grouped by stage, in the order a real engagement would touch them.

### Getting started

**W1 — Sign in and orient (3 min, every role).**
Use a quick-access card or the SSO button on the sign-in page. On the dashboard, note that every KPI card and every chart segment is clickable — they take you straight into the underlying list, pre-filtered. Hover a chart segment first to see its details. The arrow at the left of the topbar is the back button: it retraces your steps across the whole app, page by page, and its tooltip tells you where the next click lands. Open the notifications bell — entries are colour-coded by type and clicking one takes you to the exact page it came from. Finally, try the topbar search: type an audit code, a department or a person's name.

### Planning

**W2 — Plan a new audit (4 min, CAO / Director / Admin).**
Open Audit Planning. The Annual Plan tab lists every engagement — sort any column or search. Click **+ Plan New Audit** and fill in title, type, department, priority, start date, duration and budget. Under *Load from team*, pick a saved team and the lead and members fill in automatically (you can still swap individuals with the people picker). Save — the audit enters the plan with status *Planned* and the team is notified. Check the Calendar tab to see it placed, the Risk Assessment tab to see the department's score with its factor breakdown, and the Resources tab to see workload and budget update.

### Execution

**W3 — Execute an audit: checklist, evidence and tabs (6 min, Senior / Junior / CAO / Admin).**
Open Audits and click into an engagement. On the Overview tab, note the progress bar, objectives, scope and the action history — every change you make from here on lands in that timeline. Switch to the Checklist tab, click **Apply Template** and append the standard checklist, then add a custom item of your own. Set a few items to *Completed* or *In Progress* — each change records who made it. Return to Overview: the progress percentage has recalculated. Now the Evidence tab: upload a file **with a description of what it proves and the period it covers** — the description is what makes evidence useful months later. Preview it inline, download it if needed. Finish with a quick look at Work Papers and Discussion, then re-open Overview and read the complete, timestamped history of everything you just did.

**W4 — Raise a finding and track compliance (7 min, Senior + Coordinator).**
As the audit lead, open the audit workspace → Findings tab → **+ Raise Finding**. Title, category, severity, amount and response due date. Save — the department coordinator is notified automatically. Switch to the coordinator account: in the Findings Registry, open the finding and use **Submit Department Response** to record the action taken. Switch back to the lead: mark the finding *Under Review* (a remark is captured on every transition). Back in the audit workspace → Observations tab → **+ Raise Observation**, link it to the finding, state the rule violated and set a due date. The observation now appears in Compliance Tracking for time-bound follow-up — confirm it is listed with its due date and status.

**W13 — Team discussion inside an audit (2 min, audit team).**
Open any active audit → Discussion tab. Post a message — sender names and roles are shown, your own messages align right. Everything the team does in the workspace also lands in the Overview action history; nothing goes untracked.

### Review

**W7 — Review and approve an audit (6 min, Senior + CAO + Director).**
As the audit lead, open an audit that is ready for review. In the Work Papers tab, approve a submitted work paper with remarks. Open the Review tab — it shows the review history and the next step available to your role. If the reviewer is slow, use **Send Review Reminder**: pick recipients (the role above you), type the remark, send — it lands in their notifications and in the communication log. Switch to the reviewer: their dashboard lists audits pending approval. Approve with remarks (status → *Completed*). Finally the Director uses **Finalise & Issue** and the audit closes with the full review history on record.

### Reporting

**W8 — Generate and issue a report (5 min, CAO / Senior / Director / Admin).**
Open Reports → Templates and edit a template: reorder sections, change the accent colour, toggle the cover and contents pages. Back in the Reports tab, click **+ Generate Report**, pick the audit and your template. View the draft — summary, findings, workflow history — and use the download button to export it as PDF (print dialog) or Word. Advance the workflow: Send for Review → Approve (remarks required) → Mark Final & Issue. Each transition is recorded with user, time and remarks, and stakeholders are notified.

### Compliance follow-up

**W5 — Reminders, escalations and the communication log (8 min, Admin / Director / CAO / Senior).**
Open Compliance Tracking. The stat cards show overdue and escalated counts — pick an overdue observation (red "late" badge). Click the reminder bell on its row. The dialog shows the context (audit, due date) and recipient groups built from *your* role: the audit team, the department coordinator, roles above your level, same-level roles and roles below. Select recipients and type the mandatory remark — the send button stays disabled until both are provided. Send, and a toast confirms the recipient count. Open the notifications bell as one of the recipients (a second tab makes this easy): the notification is colour-coded, quotes your remark and links to the audit workspace. Now switch to the Communication Log tab: your reminder is listed with type, sender, recipients with avatars and the remark. To escalate, open an overdue observation and click Escalate — choose a level (1 Department Director → 4 Executive Board; suggested recipients switch with the level), optionally add a parallel notice to another department, type the remark and confirm. The observation moves to Escalated, the badge shows the level, and the entry lands in the communication log and the Activity Log. Section 5 below explains the rules behind all of this.

**W6 — Department coordinator workflow (4 min, Coordinator).**
Sign in as the department coordinator. The dashboard shows your department's findings awaiting response and its audits. Open the notifications bell — reminders addressed to you quote the remark and link to the audit workspace. In the Findings Registry, filter to your department, open a finding and submit the department response with action-taken details. In Compliance Tracking, your department's observations are listed with due dates, reminder counts and escalation levels — use the view icon to read the full follow-up history before responding. The Communication Log tab shows every reminder and escalation that concerns you or your department.

### Analytics

**W9 — Review flagged payments (4 min, CAO / Senior / Director / Admin).**
Open Analytics & Risk → Flagged Payments. Open a flagged transaction: the modal explains exactly which rules fired (duplicates, round figures, split purchases, weekend transfers, vendor concentration). Confirm the anomaly or mark it a false positive — with remarks; the decision is recorded in the review history. Then visit the Trends tab for monthly trends and the three-month compliance projection, and the Risk Matrix tab: hover a heat cell for details, click it to jump to the underlying audits.

### Administration and collaboration

**W10 — Manage teams and departments (4 min, CAO / Director / Admin).**
Teams: create one with the people picker (search + role filters), deactivate old ones — inactive teams cannot be assigned but keep their history. Departments: add, edit heads and budgets, deactivate; click into a profile for audit history, units, coordinators and compliance posture.

**W11 — Users, permissions and the activity trail (6 min, Admin).**
In Users & Roles, manage accounts and reset passwords. Switch to Roles & Permissions and try toggling a permission — e.g. switch *Send reminders* off for Junior Audit Officers and the reminder buttons disappear across the app immediately; switch it back. The Activity Log gives you the full trail with filters; the Reminders & Escalations tab shows the complete communication log; Login History & Sessions lists sign-ins with IP, browser and device.

**W12 — Documents and messaging (5 min, every role).**
Documents: filter by type or category, preview inline, upload new versions with a note (version history keeps every note), share with specific people — they get notified. Messages: Direct and Group threads with role and department context; open a second browser tab as another user and watch messages and notifications sync live.

## 5. Reminders, escalations and the communication log

Follow-up is where audits usually fail, so the platform is strict about it. Four rules govern everything in this section:

1. **A remark is always required.** No reminder, escalation or status decision leaves the building without a written reason. The send button stays disabled until you write one.
2. **You choose the recipients.** When you send a reminder, the dialog offers recipient groups built from *your* role: the audit team, the department coordinator, roles above your level, same-level roles and roles below. A Senior Audit Officer sees the CAO and Director above them; the CAO sees the Director above and the officers below.
3. **Everything is logged.** Every reminder and escalation — manual or automatic — is recorded in the Communication Log (Compliance Tracking → Communication Log tab, also in Activity Log → Reminders & Escalations) with sender, recipients, remark and time, and triggers a colour-coded notification that links straight to the audit.
4. **The system does its share.** Overdue observations get automatic reminders (at most every three days), and observations still open 14 days past due are auto-escalated to Level 1. These appear in the log tagged *auto* with the system as sender.

Escalation levels run from 1 to 4 — Department Director → Head of Department → Audit Director → Executive Board — and suggested recipients change with the level. You can add a parallel notice to another department when the issue crosses a boundary.

**Who may send reminders:** by default the admin, director, CAO and senior officers. An administrator can widen or narrow this in Users & Roles → Roles & Permissions — the change is immediate.

**Who sees the log:** the admin, director and CAO see everything. Everyone else sees what they sent, what was addressed to them, and everything concerning audits they are assigned to. Coordinators also see communication about their own department's audits.

## 6. Exports, printing and downloads

Every list and result set in the platform can leave it, in the format you actually need:

- **Tables** (audits, findings, compliance, communication log, activity, users, teams, documents, reports, risk scores): the **Export** button next to the table offers **CSV (.csv)** and **Excel (.xlsx)**. Excel files come with a styled header row, frozen first row and filters ready to use.
- **Reports**: the download button on a report offers **PDF (print / save)** and **Word (.doc)**.
- **Workflow documents**: in Project Guide → Workflows, use **Print all · PDF** to open a print window containing every workflow document in the app's colours (choose *Save as PDF* in the dialog), or **Word (.doc)** to download the whole set as a Word file. Each individual workflow can also be printed or downloaded on its own.
- **Pages with charts and results** (Dashboard, Analytics & Risk, Planning, Findings, Audits, Compliance): the **Print** button in the page header prints the current view — the sidebar, toolbars and buttons are left out of the print automatically.

Backups of the whole workspace (JSON) live under Settings → Data.

## 7. Good to know — small things that save time

- **The back button remembers your tab.** If you were on Analytics & Risk → Risk Matrix, clicked into an audit and press back, you return to the Risk Matrix tab — not the Overview tab. Scroll position is remembered too.
- **Two tabs are better than one.** Open the app in two browser tabs as two different users and watch messages, notifications and comments sync live — the easiest way to demo reminders.
- **Notifications are clickable.** Every notification takes you to the exact page — usually the exact audit — that raised it. The bell shows unread counts by colour.
- **Charts are clickable.** Hover any chart segment for its details; click to jump to the underlying list, filtered.
- **Your session is safe after an update.** If the workspace is ever reset, you are returned to the sign-in page with a notice instead of an error — your data re-seeds automatically on the next load.
- **Everything has a history.** Findings, observations, work papers, reports, documents and reminders each keep a complete history with who, when and remarks. When in doubt, open the history before you act.
