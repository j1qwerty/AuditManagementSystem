Decisions needed from client (SRS §6)

  1. Scope: all UP departments or a subset?
  2. Authoritative SSO / identity service?
  3. What to read/write with PFMS?
  4. Audit types and workflows in Release 1?
  5. Approval stages: observations, findings, reports?
  6. Retention and security rules for evidence?
  7. Languages (English/Hindi)?
  8. AI functions and human-only decisions?
  9. Mobile devices / OS?
  10. Content on the cut-off pages?

Non-functional requirements to define (§4)

  • Security, MFA, encryption
  • Availability, backup, DR (RTO/RPO)
  • Performance and concurrent users
  • Scalability: departments, users, documents
  • Accessibility and usability
  • Data retention and legal hold
  • AI governance and explainability
  • Interoperability standards, error/retry handling, data ownership

The SRS gives no measurable values. We recommend a workshop to fix targets before design sign-off.
